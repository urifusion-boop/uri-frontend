- **Do you offer subscription plans?**
  Yes, we offer various plans to suit your needs. Visit our pricing page to explore the options and choose the one that works best for you.

- **What subscription plans do you offer?**
  We offer three flexible subscription tiers: Starter (Free): Perfect for individuals and small businesses tracking basic brand mentions Standard: Ideal for growing businesses needing advanced analytics and reporting Enterprise: Complete solution for large organizations requiring full-scale social listening

- **Can I cancel my subscription?**
  Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings.
- **Can I upgrade my subscription?**
  Yes, you can upgrade your subscription to a higher plan whenever you want.
- **Can I change my subscription plan?**
  Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
- **Is there a free trial?**
  Yes, we offer a 7-day free trial with access to Professional features.
