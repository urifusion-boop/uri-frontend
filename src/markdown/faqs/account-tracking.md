- **What is the Account Tracking feature?**
  The Account Tracking feature enables users to monitor the performance of their social media accounts in real time. It provides key insights such as audience growth, engagement levels, and post performance across multiple platforms.

- **Who is the Account Tracking feature designed for?**
  It is designed for businesses, marketing teams, content creators, and influencers who want to optimize their social media strategy through data-driven insights.

- **What platforms are currently supported?**
  Currently, the feature supports:
  Facebook (Business Pages only)
  Instagram (Professional Accounts: Business or Creator)
  LinkedIn (Company Pages only)

- **Can I track my personal social media accounts?**
  No, only business-related accounts can be tracked. This includes Facebook Pages, Instagram Business/Creator accounts, and LinkedIn Company Pages.


- **How do I connect my social media account for tracking?**
  To connect an account:
  Navigate to the Account Tracking section on the side menu bar.
  Click "Add Account."
  Select the platform and log in with your credentials.
  Grant the necessary permissions.
  Once authentication is successful, the account will be added to your dashboard.

- **Why do I need to grant permissions?**
  Permissions allow the system to fetch data such as follower count, engagement metrics, and post analytics. Without proper authorization, tracking will not function.

- **What happens if I remove or revoke permissions for an account?**
  If permissions are revoked, tracking will be disabled until you re-authenticate the account.

- **Can I track multiple accounts at once?**
  Yes, you can track multiple social media accounts and view their performance metrics in our centralized dashboard.

- **Why is my account showing "Needs Re-Authentication"?**
  This status appears when an account's authentication has expired. Re-authenticate the account by clicking the "Reconnect" button.

- **Why is my data not updating?**
   Ensure that:
  The account is properly authenticated.
  You have granted the necessary permissions.
  There are no API restrictions from the social media platform.

- **Is my social media data secure?**
  Yes. OAuth-based authentication ensures that your credentials are secure. No passwords are stored, and all data is encrypted.

- **Can I export my account tracking data?**
  Yes. You can export your analytics data in PDF format for further analysis.

- **Who can access my account tracking data?**
  Only authorized users within your organization can access the data. No third parties have access to your insights.

- **How can I request a new feature or report an issue?**
   You can submit feature requests or report issues through the support section within the platform.
