- **What is Dera AI?**  
  Dera AI is an AI-powered data specialist designed to help users gain insights from their tracked data, including keyword tracking, account tracking, and hashtag tracking. It allows users to ask questions about their data and receive actionable insights.

- **How can I access Dera AI?**  
  Dera AI is available within the Uri platform. You can access it from the dashboard by clicking on the AI assistant chat icon or through pop-up prompts when new insights are available.

- **What kind of questions can I ask Dera AI?**  
  You can ask a variety of data-related questions, such as:  
  “What is my top-performing post, and why?”  
  “Which hashtags are driving the most engagement?”  
  “How has my account grown over the last 30 days?”  
  “Which keywords are performing poorly?”  

- **Do I need to have technical knowledge to use Dera AI?**  
  No, Dera AI is designed to be user-friendly and requires no technical expertise. You can interact with it using simple, natural language.
