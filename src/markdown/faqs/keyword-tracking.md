- **What is the Keyword Tracking feature?**
 The Keyword Tracking feature allows users to monitor specific words or phrases across multiple social media platforms. It provides insights into brand mentions, industry trends, competitor activity, and customer sentiment to support strategic decision-making.

- **How does Keyword Tracking work?**
  Simply input a keyword into the search bar, select platforms and regions for tracking, and receive real-time data on keyword mentions, engagement, reach, and sentiment analysis.

- **Which platforms does Keyword Tracking support?**
  The feature supports tracking across major social media platforms such as Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and more.

- **Can I export the analyzed results?**
  Yes, you can export the results from your Tracked Keywords and analysis. Simply go to Report Generation on the side menu bar, select keyword tracking and export your results.

- **How do keyword tracking alerts work?**
  By agreeing to receive alerts, The system detects high-value conversations and flags them for follow-up. You can check the lead tracking to see the post, alerts are updated after 1 hour of being created.

- **How can I delete a tracked keyword?**
  To delete a keyword, navigate to the Keyword Tracking dashboard, locate the keyword in your tracked list, and click the delete option. This will remove all associated data from the dashboard.
  
- **What should I do if my keyword tracking data is not updating?**
    Check your internet connection. Refresh the page. Ensure the selected platforms are supported.
    Contact support if the issue persists.
