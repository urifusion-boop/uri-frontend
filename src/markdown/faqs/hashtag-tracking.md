- **What is the Hashtag Tracking feature?**
  The Hashtag Tracking feature allows users to monitor specific hashtags and analyze key performance metrics across various social media platforms. It provides insights into the effectiveness of campaigns and helps in data-driven decision-making.

- **How do I start tracking a hashtag?**
  Navigate to the Hashtag tracking on the side menu bar, To track a hashtag, simply enter the desired hashtag in the search bar. The system will then collect and display relevant data.

- **How do I add a keyword for tracking?**
   Click “Add Keyword” and input the following details:
  Tracker Name: Custom name for identification.
  Keyword: The word or phrase to monitor.
  Set Filters: Select platforms and Geographic region if necessary
  Alerts: Opt-in for notifications when keyword activity spikes

- **Which platforms does this feature support?**
  The feature supports tracking across major social media platforms like Instagram, Facebook and Web ensuring comprehensive hashtag analysis.

- **How often is the data updated?**
  The data refresh rate is optimized for real-time or near-real-time updates to provide the most current insights.

- **How do I interpret the data from hashtag tracking?**
   The visual graphs and AI-generated reports provide clear insights into how a hashtag is performing, including engagement trends and sentiment analysis. You can use our Dera AI to further understand and ask questions about your tracked hashtags.

- **Can I export the data for further analysis?**
  Yes, users can export hashtag performance data for in-depth analysis and reporting.

- **What should I do if I encounter issues with hashtag tracking?**
  If you experience any difficulties, please check your internet connection, refresh the page, or contact support for further assistance.
