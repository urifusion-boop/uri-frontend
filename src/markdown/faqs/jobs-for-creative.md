- **Can I find jobs as a creative on Uri?**
  Yes, you can find jobs as a creative. Simply go to the Jobs section and search for opportunities that match your skills and interests.

- **How do payments work for creatives?**
  Payments are handled off the platform and are directly arranged between the client and the creative.

- **As a client, can I post job opportunities for models or   brand ambassadors on Uri creative?**
  Absolutely, Uri creative provides a platform where you can easily post job opportunities for models and brand ambassadors. It's a great way to connect with talented individuals who can represent your brand effectively.
