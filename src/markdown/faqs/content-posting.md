- **What is the Content Management feature?**
  The Content Management feature allows users to create, manage, and schedule social media posts across multiple platforms from a single interface. It helps streamline content planning, improve engagement, and optimize social media strategies.

- **Which social media platforms are supported?**
  Currently, the feature supports Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. Future updates will include support for additional platforms such as TikTok, X (formerly Twitter), and Threads.

- **Can I manage multiple social media accounts?**
 Yes, you can connect and manage multiple social media accounts under the "Platforms" tab.
  
- **How do I connect my social media accounts?**
  To connect an account:
  Navigate to the "Platforms" tab from the side menu bar
  Click "Add Account" and select the desired platform.
  Follow the authentication prompts and grant necessary permissions.
  Once connected, the account will appear in the "Platforms" list.
  
- **Is my account data secure?**
  Yes, all account connections use OAuth authentication, ensuring that your credentials remain secure. We do not store passwords, and data is accessed securely per platform API policies.
  
- **What happens if my authentication expires?**
  If authentication expires, your account will display a "Needs Re-Authentication" status in the "Platforms" tab. You must re-authenticate to resume content posting and scheduling.
  
- **How do I create a post?**
  To create a post:
  Click on the "New" button in the "Posts" tab.
  Select the platforms you want to post on.
  Enter your text and attach media (images, videos, etc.).
  Preview how the post will appear on each platform.
  Click "Publish" to post immediately or "Schedule" to post at a later time.

- **Can I preview my post before publishing?**
  Yes, the platform-specific preview feature allows you to see how your post will appear on different social media platforms before publishing.
  
- **What types of content can I post?**
  You can post:
  Reels (Instagram & Facebook)
  Images
  Text-based posts
  More coming soon

- **Can I post to multiple platforms simultaneously?**
  Yes, you can select multiple platforms when creating a post to publish the content across all chosen accounts simultaneously.

- **How do I schedule a post?**
  Navigate to the "Scheduled" tab.
  Click "New Post."
  Select the platforms, enter your content, and choose a date and time.
  Click "Schedule" to save the post for future publishing.

- **Can I edit or reschedule a post?**
  Yes, you can edit or reschedule posts before they go live. Simply click on the scheduled post in the "Scheduled" tab and make the necessary changes.

- **How do I cancel a scheduled post?**
  To cancel a scheduled post:
  Go to the "Scheduled" tab.
  Locate the post you want to cancel.
  Click on the post and select "Delete" or "Cancel Schedule."
 
- **Will I receive notifications for scheduled posts?**
  Yes, you can enable notifications to receive reminders about upcoming scheduled posts.

- **How do I move posts between sections in the "Ideas" tab?**
  You can drag and drop content ideas between sections to track progress and organize your content efficiently.

- **What is the "Ideas" tab?**
  The "Ideas" tab functions as a content brainstorming board using a Kanban-style interface with three sections:
  **To-Do**: New content ideas.
  **In Progress**: Content being developed.
  **Done**: Completed drafts ready for publishing.
